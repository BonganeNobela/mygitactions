# COMMUNICATION TOOLS FOR TEAM AND CLIENT #

## COLLABORATION ##

### 1. Why we use WhatsApp for communication within our group ### 
Our group chose WhatsApp as our primary internal communication platform because it fits the 
way we already work together day-to-day. Almost every group member already has WhatsApp 
on their phone and uses it regularly, so there’s no need to learn a new platformer having to set it 
up. This means messages, calls, updates and quick clarifications can happen instantly without 
anyone needing to log in to a new tool. 
WhatsApp also has group chats, supports voice notes and file sharing, which allows us to 
handle everything from short check-ins to quick sharing of screenshots or documents. 
Notifications are lightweight and mobile-friendly, so even when we’re away from our computers 
we can stay connected and respond quickly. In short, WhatsApp lets us communicate in real 
time, informally but efficiently, with the least friction for the team. 

### 2. Why we use Discord to communicate with the client ###

For our interactions with the client we decided on Discord rather than WhatsApp. Discord gives 
us a slightly more structured environment and a clearer separation between our internal 
conversations and client facing communication. With Discord we can create a dedicated 
channel for us to communicate solely with a client when needed. 
It also provides better tools for hosting scheduled calls, screen sharing, and sharing larger files 
than WhatsApp does. This is especially useful when we’re walking the client through new 
features, gathering feedback, or running live demos. By keeping client communication on 
Discord, we maintain a professional yet accessible space for them to reach us, while protecting 
the privacy and informality of our internal WhatsApp chats.
