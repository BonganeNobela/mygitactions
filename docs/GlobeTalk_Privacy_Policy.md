# GlobeTalk Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 19 October 2025  

At **GlobeTalk**, your privacy and safety are our top priorities.  
Our goal is to create a safe, inclusive space where people around the world can connect through virtual pen pal conversations — without compromising their personal information.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information needed to make GlobeTalk work safely and meaningfully.

### a. Profile Information
When you create an account, you may provide:
- Your preferred language  
- Age range  
- Region or approximate timezone (e.g., “South Africa (GMT+2)”)  
- Hobbies or interests  
- A short bio or cultural introduction  

> ⚠️ We **do not** collect real names, phone numbers, email addresses, or precise location data.

### b. Matchmaking Data
To find compatible pen pals, we use:
- Language and region preferences  

No personally identifying information is shared during the matchmaking process.

### c. Messages
- Messages are text-only and stored securely.  
- Messages are private between matched users.  
- Moderators can review flagged messages **only** for rule violations.

### d. Moderation Data
If you report a user or message, GlobeTalk logs:
- The report reason  
- The message or user ID involved  
- The moderation outcome (e.g., warning, suspension)  

This helps maintain community safety.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data to:
- Match you with suitable pen pals  
- Deliver and store messages safely  
- Enforce safety and moderation rules  
- Improve the app’s performance and experience  

We **do not sell, rent, or share** your data with third parties for marketing.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Security
- All communications are encrypted during transmission.  
- User identities are hidden — we use unique IDs instead of real names or contact info.  
- Moderators can only view content necessary for rule enforcement.  
- Access to stored data is restricted to authorized personnel.

---

## 4. Anonymity & Safety
GlobeTalk is designed for anonymous cultural exchange:
- No sharing of personal contact details (emails, phone numbers, social media, etc.)  
- No media or file sharing — text-only messages  
- Users can block or report others at any time  
- Inappropriate content is reviewed and may lead to suspension or removal

---

## 5. Your Rights
You can:
- View or edit your profile details  
- Delete your account and data permanently  
- Request a copy of your message history  
- Report unsafe behavior or content

---

## 6. Data Retention
- Messages and match records are retained as long as the account is active.  
- Deleted accounts are permanently removed from our database within 30 days.  
- Moderation logs may be kept longer for safety and compliance reasons.

---

## 7. Children’s Privacy
GlobeTalk is intended for users aged **18 and older**.  
We do not knowingly collect information from minors under 16.  
If such data is discovered, it will be deleted immediately.

---

## 8. Updates to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect new features or legal requirements.  
Any significant changes will be communicated through the app or our website.
