# PROJECT MANAGEMENT TOOL – NOTION 

## 1. Why use Notion 
Our group uses Notion because it gives us a single, flexible workspace where we can organise 
everything related to the project. Instead of juggling multiple apps for notes, documents, and task lists, Notion lets us keep all of that in one place. This makes it easy for everyone to find the 
latest version of meeting notes, project requirements, or deadlines without digging through 
endless chats or emails. 
Notion also helps us stay on the same page about responsibilities. With shared task boards and 
checklists, we can see who is working on what and track progress together. The fact that it’s 
cloud-based means any update, whether someone adds a new task, edits a document, or 
checks off a task is instantly visible to the whole group. 
Another big reason we like Notion is how simple it is to customise. We’ve been able to set up 
pages that actually fit how we work, from a central project dashboard down to quick task lists.
This flexibility makes it feel more like our workspace rather than just another generic tool. 
In short, Notion helps us stay organised, reduce confusion, and work more smoothly as a team 
without wasting time searching for scattered information. 


## 2. Pros of using Notion as a Project Management Tool 
 
• Cost-effective – As an all in one platform, Notion reduces the need to invest in separate 
tools for CRM, onboarding, and project management.  
• Easy to use – Once you get familiar with its features, creating pages and managing 
content becomes simpler compared to many other platforms.  
• Efficient – With built-in templates, shortcuts, and powerful features for note taking, 
task assignment, and workflow automation, Notion helps teams save time. Since 
everything can be done within one workspace, there’s no need to constantly switch 
between apps.  
• Accessible anywhere – Notion works seamlessly across devices, whether on web, 
desktop, or mobile, making it easy for users to stay connected and productive from any 
location.
