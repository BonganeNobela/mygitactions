## 1. Purpose of GlobeTalk
GlobeTalk provides a safe, text-only environment where users can:

- Share cultural insights, hobbies, and experiences respectfully  

Our mission is to promote global curiosity, friendship, and cultural understanding.

We encourage you to protect your privacy by avoiding self-identifying information.
