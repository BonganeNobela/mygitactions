    GlobeTalk Terms of Service

    Last updated: 19 October 2025

    Welcome to GlobeTalk, a virtual pen pal platform designed to connect people across the world through meaningful, anonymous cultural exchanges.

    By creating an account or using GlobeTalk, you agree to these Terms of Service.
    Please read them carefully.

    1. Purpose of GlobeTalk

    GlobeTalk provides a safe, text-only environment where users can: - Match with pen pals based on language and region preferences
    - Exchange delayed text messages (simulating postal letters)
    - Share cultural insights, hobbies, and experiences respectfully

    Our mission is to promote global curiosity, friendship, and cultural understanding.

    2. Eligibility

    • You must be 18 years or older to use GlobeTalk.
    • You may not use GlobeTalk if you have been previously banned.
    • By registering, you confirm that the information you provide (e.g., language, region, bio) is truthful and non-identifying.

    3. User Responsibilities

    You agree to: - Treat others with respect and kindness.
    - Keep all communication appropriate and culturally sensitive.
    - Never share personal contact information (e.g., phone numbers, email, social media).
    - Avoid using offensive, discriminatory, or explicit language.
    - Report any inappropriate or unsafe behavior through the in-app tools.

    You are solely responsible for the messages and content you send through the platform.

    4. Prohibited Conduct

    You may not use GlobeTalk to: - Harass, threaten, or bully other users
    - Impersonate another person or share false information
    - Send explicit, violent, or hateful messages
    - Attempt to exchange external contact details or meet users outside the platform
    - Spam or advertise products or services
    - Circumvent moderation systems or exploit platform bugs

    Violations of these rules may lead to temporary suspension or permanent account termination.

    5. Anonymity and Safety

    GlobeTalk is designed for anonymous cultural exchange: - Real names, photos, and personal contact details are not required or shared.
    - Messages are text-only (no media, images, or files).
    - Each user is identified only by a username and user ID.
    - You can block or report users at any time if you feel uncomfortable.

    We encourage you to protect your privacy by avoiding self-identifying information.

    6. Message Delays & Availability

    • GlobeTalk messages may be delayed to simulate the experience of postal mail.
    • Delays can also occur due to moderation or server load.
    • We do not guarantee constant availability or delivery times, though we aim for reliability.

    7. Moderation & Reporting

    To maintain community safety: - All reports are reviewed by moderators.
    - Repeated or severe violations may lead to a permanent ban.
    - Moderators may access reported messages only to review for violations.
    - We may remove content or accounts that violate these Terms or harm the community.

    8. Account Termination

    We reserve the right to suspend or delete accounts that: - Violate these Terms or our Privacy Policy
    - Are reported for harmful behavior
    - Are inactive for extended periods

    You may delete your account at any time through the Settings page.
    Deleted data is permanently removed within 30 days.

    9. Intellectual Property

    • GlobeTalk, its logo, and its interface are owned by the GlobeTalk team.
    • You retain rights to the messages and bios you write but grant GlobeTalk a limited license to store and display them within the app.

    10. Limitation of Liability

    GlobeTalk is provided “as is” without warranties of any kind.
    We are not responsible for: - The content of user messages
    - Interactions between users outside the app
    - Technical interruptions or data loss

    However, we take every reasonable measure to ensure a safe and reliable experience.

    11. Privacy and Data

    Your use of GlobeTalk is also governed by our Privacy Policy, which explains: - What data we collect
    - How we store and protect it
    - How you can control or delete your data

    12. Changes to the Terms

    We may update these Terms to reflect new features or regulations.
    If we make significant changes, we will notify users through the app or by email.
